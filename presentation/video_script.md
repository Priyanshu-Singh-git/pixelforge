# Video script: PixelForge (60–80 s)

Record with OBS, 1080p, cursor zoom on clicks, burned-in captions. Open the demo first so the model is cached:
https://priyanshu-singh-git.github.io/pixelforge/

| Time | On screen | Say (casually) |
|---|---|---|
| 0–8 s | Brick texture loaded; drag the slider slowly from left to right | "Same old game texture: blurry and blocky on the left, PixelForge 4× on the right." |
| 8–25 s | Click 2 more samples (Aerial Rocks, Clay Floor); drag the slider | "I trained this from scratch on hundreds of free game textures, damaged on purpose so it learns to undo blur, noise and compression." |
| 25–40 s | Click **vs True HD**, drag the slider | "Here's the real HD original next to it. It's honest: it cleans the texture up a lot, but it can't invent detail that was never there." |
| 40–55 s | Click **…or upload your own**, pick a texture from your PC; click **Download HD PNG** | "Upload your own. It runs in the browser, nothing gets uploaded. Download the 4× file." |
| 55–68 s | Scroll to **How good is it?** table | "On sixty textures it never saw, it beats the official Real-ESRGAN model while being fifteen times smaller." |
| 68–80 s | Back to the top | "Link's below. If you're doing an HD mod or a remaster, send me ten of your textures and I'll send them back in HD." |

Tip: use a texture from a game you own only for a private test; for the public video, stick to the demo samples or your own art.
